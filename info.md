My name is Vedran Sminderovac and I was born in 1985 in Zagreb, where I live and work.

Interest in photography has been evident throughout my childhood, although I did not fully understand these light stimuli until the popularization of digital cameras.

I found myself as a street and life photographer. I love street photography because it is hard to imagine and unrepeatable, from subjects and backdrops to situations that only life can direct.

I would highlight Henri Cartier-Bresson, Elliott Erwitt, Robert Doisneau and Bruno Barbey as inspiration. Their photographs have an aesthetic and sensibility that I myself strive for, one could say – a humanistic approach to the urban world.

## Solo exhibitions

- 2026 *street=stage*, Kraljice Katarina Kosača Gallery, Mostar (organized by Fotoklub Mostar)
- 2024 *Adriatic. As Once Was*, Botaničar bar and gallery, Zagreb
- 2023 *Street Geometry*, Oblok Gallery, Sesvete
- 2023 *Street Geometry*, Fotoklub Split Gallery
- 2023 *Street Geometry*, Fotoklub Zagreb Gallery
- 2022 *Street Geometry*, Vladimir Horvat Gallery, Zagreb

I have also exhibited at numerous group exhibitions and competitions around the world.

I have been a member of Fotoklub Zagreb since 2021.
