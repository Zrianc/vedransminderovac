Vedran Sminderovac (Zagreb, 1985) is a photographer who lives and works in Zagreb, Croatia.
He has been interested in photography since childhood and has pursued it seriously since 2020.
He has been a member of Fotoklub Zagreb since 2021.

He is drawn above all to street photography — the strange, unrepeatable moments of everyday life.
He deliberately brings architecture into the frame for its geometry and order, so his photographs
are minimalist, restrained in colour and contrast, and free of unnecessary detail.
His role models are Henri Cartier-Bresson and Stanko Abadžić.
