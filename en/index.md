Vedran Sminderovac is a photographer from Zagreb, Croatia. He works in street photography —
looking for strange, unrepeatable moments of everyday life and the order the city hides in its geometry.

He shoots in both black and white and colour. His work has been exhibited in Zagreb and Split
and awarded at national and international photography salons.
